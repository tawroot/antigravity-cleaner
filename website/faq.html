Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common issues and safety questions.

How do I bypass Google Antigravity Error 403 Forbidden / Region Lock?

Run antigravity-cleaner patch or click [Auto-Fix] in the Retro GUI. This neutralizes client-side restriction checks in main.js, agy, and extensions. Then launch with antigravity-cleaner launch to route the IDE through your local proxy without TUN mode.

How to resolve Error 429 Quota Exceeded without losing chats?

Click [Reset 429] in the GUI or run antigravity-cleaner clean. It surgically resets rate-limit blobs and token cache while keeping your Google login session, cookies, and chat history 100% intact.

Does it work without VPN TUN mode?

Yes! Antigravity Cleaner uses direct Smart Proxy Injection (--proxy-server=socks5://127.0.0.1:). Your domestic internet and banking apps stay fast on direct connection, while only Antigravity routes through your proxy.

Is it safe to use?

Yes. Antigravity Cleaner is 100% open-source, runs offline on your local computer, contains zero telemetry, and never touches your personal project source code.

Will I lose my browser passwords?

No. Our "Session Preservation" technology ensures that critical files like Cookies and Login Data are backed up or excluded from the cleaning process, so you don't have to log in again.

How do I fix "Network Error"?

Use the "Network Reset" feature in the Tools menu. This will flush your DNS cache and reset the Winsock catalog, which often resolves connection issues caused by VPNs using the Tun/Tap adapter.

Do you support Mac M1/M2/M3/M4?

Yes, we have native builds for Apple Silicon (ARM64) as well as Intel Macs. Check the Download page for the correct version.
